About Me

I’m a Senior AI Engineer specializing in computer vision, LLMs and AI agents, and speech AI. I build and deploy models with Python, PyTorch and TensorFlow, and ship them as production APIs.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BUBT (CGPA 3.97) and work as a Senior AI Engineer at Omicon Group, after more than two years at HawkEyes Digital Monitoring Limited. My work spans computer vision (classification, detection, segmentation, pose estimation and object tracking), AI agents, LLMs and fine-tuning, speech technologies (TTS, STT, speech recognition, audio diarization and speech model fine-tuning), and pattern recognition.
